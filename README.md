# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive excel project designed to analyze employee attrition and workforce trends. The dashboard provides insights into employee demographics, education, job roles, departments, age groups, marital status, and job satisfaction to help organizations improve employee retention.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition across the organization.
- Identify departments with the highest employee turnover.
- Evaluate attrition by job role.
- Understand the relationship between education and attrition.
- Analyze attrition across different age groups.
- Compare employee attrition by marital status.
- Monitor important HR KPIs.
- Support data-driven HR decision-making.

---

### 🎓 Education-Based Attrition

| Education Level | Attrition Count |
| --- | ---: |

---

### 💼 Attrition by Job Role

The highest employee attrition is observed in the following job roles:

- Laboratory Technician
- Sales Executive
- Research Scientist
- Sales Representative
- Manufacturing Director
- Healthcare Representative

**Insights:**

- Technical and sales positions experience the highest turnover.
- Employee engagement strategies should focus on these roles.

---

### 🏢 Department-Wise Attrition

Departments included in the analysis:

- Human Resources
- Research and Development (R&D)
- Sales

**Insights:**

- Sales and R&D departments contribute significantly to employee attrition.
- Department-level analysis helps identify high-risk areas.
